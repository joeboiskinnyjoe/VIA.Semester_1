A little about myself

My name is Lukas, and I am a first-semester Software Engineering student at VIA University College in Horsens, Denmark. I have been interested in technology for most of my life, and I am motivated by the potential of software development to solve problems, create new opportunities, and shape the future.

I consider myself a curious person who enjoys learning new things and challenging myself. Although I am still at the beginning of my programming journey, I am eager to develop my skills, expand my knowledge, and gain practical experience. My long-term ambition is to become a software engineer who contributes to meaningful technological advancements.

Outside my studies, I have a wide range of interests. I have practised Taekwondo since the age of five and hold a black belt. Through years of training, I have developed discipline, perseverance, and a strong appreciation for continuous improvement. I have also played golf for much of my life and participated in competitive rifle shooting.

Creativity is another important part of my life. I taught myself to play guitar at the age of eleven and piano at fourteen. I also enjoy cooking, experimenting with desserts, and playing video games, both independently and with friends.

I believe that my combination of technical curiosity, discipline, and creativity provides a strong foundation for my development as a future software engineer. I look forward to applying these qualities throughout my education and future career.
